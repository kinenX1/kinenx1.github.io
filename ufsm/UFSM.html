UFSM

Six planes, eight airports, three countries. Weather and time of day change every flight. Pick your plane, pick where you take off and where you land. Fly the gold marker, then follow the gold approach lights down to the runway.

PC
W/S pitch, A/D bank, Shift/Ctrl throttle, G gear, F flaps, Z spoilers, Space gun, B nuke, M map, H help. In battle: WASD move, mouse aims, click fires, Space jumps, R reloads, 1-7 or Q/E weapons, Tab score.
THROTTLE
Slide up to speed up, all the way down to brake on the ground.
STICK
Drag down to climb, up to dive, sideways to turn. Let go and the plane levels itself.
GEAR
Raise the Embraer's wheels after takeoff, lower them before landing. Land without them and you slide on the belly.
CAMERA
One free chase camera: drag the screen to swing it around the plane, pinch to zoom, double-tap to reset.
FAILURES
Engines quit, fires start, hydraulics and electrics fail. Tap FAIL to set one off yourself, and use the buttons in the red alerts to handle them.
F-14
The wings sweep back on their own as you speed up. Push the throttle past 90% for afterburner and break the sound barrier.
FLAPS
Lever with 5 levels: UP, 1, 2, 3, FULL. Drag it down for more flaps and slower flight. "FLAP SPEED" means you are too fast for that level.
SPOILERS
Lever with RET, ARM and 4 levels. ARM before landing and they pop up on touchdown. Levels 1 to 4 slow you down in the air.
CHUTE
Fighters can pull a drag chute after landing to stop faster.
MAP
Tap the small map in the corner to open the big satellite map. Tap an airport on it to fly there.
SERVICE
Stop on any runway to refuel and repair.
AUTOLAND
Tap AUTOPILOT in SYS until it says LAND. The plane flies the approach and lands by itself.
EJECT
Fighters have an ejection seat in SYS. The pilot floats down under a parachute.
GUN
Fighters fire tracers with the red GUN button. Use it in Target Practice.
NUKE
Only the F-22 carries nuclear bombs, 3 per flight. Drop from high up and climb away: the blast destroys everything within about 650 m. The F-5 drops small bombs.
RADAR
The Cessna has a radar. It shows every plane and tank around you and warns your team over the radio.
CRASHES
Hit the ground too hard, a building, a wind turbine or another plane and the jet breaks apart.
SYS
Autopilot, flaps, spoilers, airshow smoke, voice and photo mode.
CRASHES
Hitting the ground damages the plane but you keep going. Too much damage kills the engine.

Plays in portrait or landscape. Landscape gives the widest view.
