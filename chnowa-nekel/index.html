What it does

🎯
Find my dish7 quick questions: meal, people, budget, time, mood, what to avoid and what's in your kitchen.
📖
845 recipes58 Tunisian dishes, including Sfax specials like kosksi bel hout and charmoula, plus 787 dishes from 37 countries, with photos.
💸
Your budget in DTType how much you want to spend. Each dish shows its cost based on Sfax market prices.
🛒
Smart shopping listSorted by shop (butcher, fish, vegetables…). Send it on WhatsApp in one tap.
📍
Shops near youGoogle Maps shows butchers, fish markets, bakeries and supermarkets around you.
🤖
Chef AIAsk anything in English, French, Arabic or Derja: "I have chicken, 15 DT, 3 people".

How to install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android at the top of this page.
  2. Open the downloaded file ChnowaNekel.apk (from the notification or your Files app).
  3. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then tap Install.
  4. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway. This happens because the app isn't on the Play Store yet.
📱 iPhone? iPhones can't install APK files. Use the browser version and add it to your home screen from the Share menu.
